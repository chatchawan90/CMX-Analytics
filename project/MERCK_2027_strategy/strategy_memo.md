# Merck 2027 revenue growth strategy

Prepared for the CMX and Merck partner planning meeting • 20 November 2026

## Recommendation

We propose a **THB 129.64m working revenue target for 2027**, equivalent to 10% growth over an estimated THB 117.86m full-year 2026 baseline. Build the plan around protecting large Chemistry accounts, recovering declining existing business, and expanding analytical workflows in accounts we already serve. Use selective new-customer acquisition to supplement those priorities.

The central issue is concentration, not a lack of customer acquisition. Revenue is up 5.6% year to date, but **PTT Global Chemical and 3M contributed THB 5.39m of the THB 5.55m net increase**. Excluding both accounts from both comparison periods, growth is only **0.17%**. We should secure the repeatability of those gains before treating them as a foundation for further growth.

Take three scenarios to the meeting: **THB 123.75m conservative, THB 129.64m base, and THB 135.54m supported stretch**. These are planning targets, not probability-weighted forecasts or commitments. The base case assumes current resources; the stretch case requires specific support from Merck. Validate the account opportunities and replace the estimated baseline with full-year actuals before committing the annual target.

**Basis:** Merck-family categorized invoice revenue, before VAT, through 20 November 2026; excludes invoices marked `บิลปลอม` and future-dated records. All monetary values below are THB millions unless stated otherwise. The export remains provisional because category filtering, document scope and invoice-line deduplication have not been reconciled to source sales. This is a Merck-family plan, not a company-wide revenue plan.

## Which categories and subcategories to push

Prioritize **Analytical Chemistry and Solvents**, **FB&O Microbiology**, and **Chemical Synthesis and Discovery Chemistry** for expansion. Their growth is supported by buyer breadth and remains positive after excluding each subcategory's largest current buyer. Use **Reference Materials as a qualified cross-sell**: revenue grew 7.5%, but its buyer count fell from 193 to 185. Pilot **Cell and General Workflow Tools** before scaling; only 16 of its 53 buyers have multiple invoices.

Keep recovery distinct from growth campaigns: focus on **Protein and Pathway Analysis**, **Cell Biology Reagents**, and **Pharma Microbiology**. Maintain other specialist opportunities selectively. Do not extrapolate Analytical Chromatography's 440.8% growth: one customer contributes 93.4% of its revenue.

The [category push playbook](category_push_playbook.md) ranks the subcategories, identifies target customer groups, and specifies actions and Merck support requests. This is the portfolio decision layer; account priorities implement it, and their revenue must not be counted twice.

## What the sales evidence says

Comparable periods are 1 January through 20 November in each year.

| Business | 2025 YTD | 2026 YTD | Change | Growth |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Chemistry | 61.92 | 70.38 | +8.46 | +13.7% |
| Biology | 25.80 | 22.65 | −3.15 | −12.2% |
| Biomonitoring | 8.05 | 8.42 | +0.37 | +4.6% |
| Diagnostics and Regulated Materials | 2.73 | 2.60 | −0.13 | −4.8% |
| **Total** | **98.50** | **104.05** | **+5.55** | **+5.6%** |

Chemistry accounts for 67.6% of current revenue. Analytical Chemistry and Solvents generated THB 50.60m, up 15.7%; Reference Materials generated THB 6.70m, up 7.5%. These are the clearest adjacent categories for account expansion, subject to application fit.

Biology recovery requires focused account work. Protein and Pathway Analysis is down THB 1.18m, and Cell Biology Reagents is down THB 0.46m. A further THB 1.85m decline sits in unclassified Biology—about 59% of the category's net decline—so map those products before allocating the full recovery target by subcategory. University customers across all categories are down THB 4.03m, or 13.1%; their Biology purchases still represent THB 12.24m. The data shows where demand fell, but cannot establish whether the cause was funding, pricing, competition, availability or project timing.

Biomonitoring should be selective: FB&O Microbiology grew 17.5%, while Pharma Microbiology fell 22.8%. Customer industry and product category are different dimensions; the FB&O category's growth does not prove that our Food and Beverage industry segment grew—it declined 9.6%.

## Commercial priorities

### 1 Protect large accounts and expand Chemistry workflows

Start with PTT Global Chemical (`ZH-PTTGC`), 3M Innovation Thailand (`ZH-MMMI`), Chulabhorn Research Institute (`ZH-CRI`) and Thai Polyethylene (`ZH-SCG-TPE`). Confirm purchasing calendars, application requirements, repeat-order expectations and supply needs. PTT purchased on 81 invoices across 11 months; 3M purchased on seven invoices across three months. That makes 3M's ordering cadence particularly important to validate; it does not prove the business is one-off.

The first-wave core-account list contains 20 accounts representing THB 42.16m of 2026 YTD sales, excluding accounts already assigned to the Biology recovery list. Account owners should document retention risks and expansion opportunities before asking Merck for general price concessions.

For expansion, qualify Reference Materials opportunities among analytical chemistry buyers. There are **341 accounts with Analytical Chemistry and Solvents purchases but no recorded Reference Materials purchases**. Begin with the 15 selected candidates in the account appendix. Absence of purchases is a qualification signal, not evidence of unmet demand or a revenue forecast.

### 2 Recover declining Biology and other existing-account demand

Review the 15 selected Biology accounts first. Their combined gross Biology decline is THB 4.80m; this exceeds the category's net decline because other accounts grew. Initial priorities include `ZH-RM2` (Ramathibodi/Chakri Naruebodindra), `ZH-MU-PH` (Mahidol Pharmacy), `ZH-CRA` (Chulabhorn Royal Academy), and `ZH-BIOT` (BIOTEC).

Each review should identify the lost applications and products, compare purchasing timing, confirm the reason for decline, and define the next commercial action. Use a technical discussion where application fit or method performance is the issue; use a supply or commercial discussion only when the account evidence supports it.

Across all active accounts, the estimated shortfall against their own full-year 2025 revenue is **THB 21.46m**. This is a historical recovery pool using a common seasonal estimate, not qualified pipeline. The base scenario assumes recovery of 35%, or THB 7.51m, across categories. Account qualification must establish whether that assumption is achievable; the 15-account Biology list alone does not substantiate it.

### 3 Reactivate selectively and acquire lookalike customers

There are **149 full-year 2025 buyers** with no 2026 purchases through the cutoff, representing THB 3.72m of 2025 revenue. This differs from the 138 comparable-YTD inactive accounts because it also includes buyers who purchased only after 20 November 2025. Check late-year buying patterns before classifying an account as lost.

Start with 15 inactive accounts representing THB 1.85m of prior full-year revenue, excluding accounts already assigned to Biology recovery. The base case assumes THB 0.93m of reactivated revenue across the full inactive pool. Confirm the buyer, project status and purchasing channel before offering incentives.

For new customers, prioritize analytical laboratory-service profiles: 31 laboratory-service customers bought Chemistry, and 28 had at least two invoices. This provides a stronger repeat-purchase signal than extrapolating from the six Consumer Goods customers or eight Petroleum customers. Use the existing customer profile to qualify prospects; no external prospect list or market-size claim is implied.

## Revenue targets and the work required to reach them

The 2026 full-year estimate is `104.050721 × (111.574551 / 98.503852) = 117.857447`. Using 2024 seasonality instead gives THB 116.71m. Neither estimate adjusts for account-specific order timing; confirm major-account plans and refresh after year-end. The 10% target is THB 128.38m using the alternative seasonal baseline, versus THB 129.64m using the selected baseline.

| Estimated 2026 starting revenue | 117.86 | 117.86 | 117.86 |
| Allowance for contraction and attrition | −4.71 | −3.54 | −2.36 |

| **Gross positive revenue required before losses** | **10.61** | **15.32** | **20.04** |

These allocations are assumptions for discussion. Revenue retention is set at 96% / 97% / 98%; recovery of the active-account shortfall at 20% / 35% / 50%; and recovery of inactive-account prior revenue at 15% / 25% / 35%. The remaining target requirement is split between existing expansion and new customers at 75:25 / 75:25 / 70:30. These are not measured conversion probabilities.

**The base plan still needs THB 15.32m of positive initiatives to be validated.** Invoice history contains no qualified 2027 opportunity pipeline; this is not a claim that CMX has no pipeline. Recover lost account spend first in the bridge, and count only spending above the recovery ceiling as additional expansion. Record each incremental opportunity once across category, industry and initiative views.

## Execution and review

The following are proposed actions and accountable roles, not confirmed individual assignments. The account appendix includes the latest recorded salesperson only as a routing aid; the sales manager must confirm ownership.

| Period | Execution priorities | Base revenue target |
|---|---|---:|
| Q1 | Confirm full-year baseline; review all 65 priority accounts; validate recovery reasons, core account plans and support requests | 30.96 |

Quarter targets use the 2025 revenue seasonality of 23.88%, 24.23%, 26.14% and 25.75%; they are planning allocations, not contractual delivery forecasts.

By **29 January 2027**, the sales manager should confirm owners and next actions for all 65 accounts, distinguish reorder timing from true decline, and refresh the baseline with full-year 2026 actuals. By **26 February**, sales leads should replace historical opportunity signals with account-specific incremental amounts, expected order dates and evidence of customer interest. Where the bridge remains unsupported, show the gap rather than assigning the difference to an unnamed campaign.

Review monthly: revenue against seasonal target; retention of 2026 account revenue; realized recovery and reactivation; new-customer revenue; Reference Materials first orders; and qualified incremental pipeline by expected order quarter. Use a provisional **3× unweighted pipeline coverage** requirement for remaining gross growth, clearly marked as an operating assumption until actual win rates are available. Track budget and capacity requirements alongside opportunities without treating proposed discounts or additional headcount as approved.

Meet with Merck quarterly to review commitments, revenue conversion and unresolved supply/application issues. If either large growth account cannot sustain its 2026 purchases, or the base bridge lacks qualified opportunities, revise the forecast before increasing the target. Each one-percentage-point change in revenue retention changes the revenue requirement by approximately THB 1.18m.
