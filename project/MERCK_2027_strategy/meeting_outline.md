# Merck 2027 partner meeting outline

Purpose: agree a revenue-growth direction, a provisional target, and the support needed to convert account opportunities into orders. Suggested duration: 45 minutes, including discussion. This is a slide-by-slide outline, not a finished presentation.

All revenue figures are THB millions, before VAT. YTD comparisons end on 20 November. Exclude `บิลปลอม` invoices and future-dated transactions. Label the categorized-export baseline provisional on the performance and target slides.

## Slide 1 Agree the growth ambition and conditions

**Message:** We propose THB 129.64m as the 2027 working target, with a THB 135.54m stretch conditional on Merck support and validated opportunities.

Show three target cards: conservative 123.75, base 129.64, supported stretch 135.54. Put “planning scenarios, not committed forecasts” directly underneath. The estimated 2026 baseline is 117.86.

**Say:** “We want to grow the business through repeatable account expansion and recovery. Today we want agreement on priorities, support and the process for validating the target.”

**Ask:** Can both teams use the base case as the working direction, pending full-year actuals and account validation?

## Slide 2 Growth is concentrated and must be protected

**Message:** Revenue is up 5.6%, but two accounts explain almost the entire net increase.

Show a three-year comparable-YTD chart: 2024 100.91; 2025 98.50; 2026 104.05. Add a concentration panel: PTT Global Chemical and 3M contributed 5.39 of the 5.55 net increase; excluding both from both years, growth is 0.17%. Top-ten accounts contribute 30.3% of revenue.

**Say:** “Chemistry is growing, but the headline rate does not demonstrate broad-based growth. We need to confirm repeat orders at our largest growth accounts and expand the rest of the portfolio.”

**Ask:** Which Merck counterparts can help us validate critical products, supply and 2027 order plans for these accounts?

**Evidence:** `analysis_summary.json`; `account_category_changes.csv`; `account_opportunities.csv`. Do not describe 3M's business as one-off: seven invoices in three months is a signal to investigate, not a cause established by the data.

## Slide 3 Expand Chemistry and recover Biology selectively

**Message:** Chemistry adds 8.46, while Biology offsets 3.15 of that growth.

Show category revenue and growth: Chemistry 70.38 / +13.7%; Biology 22.65 / −12.2%; Biomonitoring 8.42 / +4.6%; Diagnostics and Regulated Materials 2.60 / −4.8%.

Highlight Analytical Chemistry and Solvents +15.7%, Reference Materials +7.5%, Protein and Pathway Analysis −18.3%, and Cell Biology Reagents −9.5%. For Biomonitoring, contrast FB&O Microbiology +17.5% with Pharma Microbiology −22.8%.

**Recommendation to show:** Push Analytical Chemistry and Solvents, FB&O Microbiology, and Chemical Synthesis and Discovery Chemistry. Attach Reference Materials as a qualified cross-sell. Pilot Cell and General Workflow Tools. Recover Protein and Pathway Analysis, Cell Biology Reagents, and Pharma Microbiology separately. See the [ranked category push playbook](category_push_playbook.md).

**Say:** “We have selected specific subcategories for growth, rather than treating all Chemistry or Biology products alike. Buyer breadth and repeat demand support the first three priorities; Reference Materials needs cross-selling to rebuild breadth, and Workflow Tools needs a repeatability pilot.”

**Caution to show:** THB 1.85m of Biology's THB 3.15m net decline is unclassified. Ask Merck to help map those products before assigning the full recovery target. Analytical Chromatography's 440.8% growth is concentrated 93.4% in one customer and does not justify a broad campaign.

**Ask:** Agree the product specialists and priority applications for the account reviews.

**Evidence:** `category_subcategory_priorities.csv`; `subcategory_industry_evidence.csv`. Do not add category and subcategory opportunities together: these are views of the same revenue.

## Slide 4 Existing accounts are the main opportunity

**Message:** Retained-account growth is strong, but retained-account contraction consumes much of it.

Show the historical revenue bridge: 98.50 starting revenue +24.51 retained-account expansion −18.89 retained-account contraction −3.41 inactive buyers +3.32 additional buyers =104.05.

Use a second panel for the first wave: 20 core accounts, 15 Biology recovery accounts, 15 inactive accounts, and 15 Reference Materials qualification accounts. These are 65 distinct account codes. Only 118 current buyers are first observed in 2026; they generated 1.99, not the entire 3.32 additional-buyer amount.

**Say:** “We have prioritized specific accounts for review. We have not converted historic declines or absent purchases into confirmed pipeline.”

**Ask:** Confirm current owners and agree the first-wave reviews by 29 January 2027.

**Evidence:** `priority_accounts.csv`; `account_opportunities.csv`. The account appendix contains organization names and latest recorded salesperson names for routing. Confirm account ownership before presenting assignments as final.

## Slide 5 Turn account evidence into commercial actions

**Message:** Use a small number of measurable programs, with selective acquisition.

Show four action cards:

- **Protect and expand core accounts:** confirm the order plans of PTT, 3M, Chulabhorn Research Institute and Thai Polyethylene; qualify adjacent analytical workflows.
- **Recover Biology and other declines:** diagnose reasons, identify products/applications, and define an account-specific next step. The 15 Biology accounts show 4.80 of gross YTD decline.
- **Reactivate prior buyers:** validate the 15-account initial list, representing 1.85 of prior full-year revenue, before agreeing any incentive.
- **Qualify selective expansion and acquisition:** review 15 Reference Materials candidates; use laboratory-service profiles for prospect qualification. Chemistry has 31 laboratory-service buyers, 28 with repeat invoices.

**Ask:** Agree program leads and the evidence required to count an opportunity: account, product/application, incremental amount, next action, expected order date and customer-interest evidence.

**Evidence:** `priority_accounts.csv`; `industry_category_opportunities.csv`. Industry assignments are available for 84.7% of revenue and are from the May 2026 snapshot.

## Slide 6 Make the revenue bridge explicit

**Message:** The 10% target requires 15.32 of positive initiatives after allowing for 3.54 of losses.

Show the base bridge: 117.86 estimated starting revenue −3.54 losses +7.51 active-account recovery +0.93 reactivation +5.16 additional expansion +1.72 new customers =129.64. Beside it, show the conservative and supported-stretch totals of 123.75 and 135.54.

**Say:** “The target is an ambition that must be backed by accounts. We used explicit recovery and retention assumptions, not historic opportunity conversion rates. All 15.32 of the base positive bridge needs validation against our actual pipeline.”

**Ask:** Agree the assumptions to validate and the decision date for the committed target.

**Evidence:** `revenue_scenarios.csv`; `methodology.md`. Full-year 2026 estimate is 117.86 using 2025 seasonality, or 116.71 using 2024 seasonality. The actual year-end result replaces both estimates. A one-point retention change shifts the requirement by about 1.18.

Show the support-to-outcome allocation: major-account supply/commercial review 1.18; increased active-account recovery 3.22; reactivation 0.37; additional expansion 0.44; new customers 0.68.

Request 12 joint Q1 application/account reviews, two Biology/application clinics and two analytical workflow sessions by end-Q2, named counterparts, and account-specific supply/commercial decisions. Support requests are proposals; the amounts are allocations of the scenario difference, not proven returns on events or specialist time.

**Ask:** Record each commitment as agreed, under review or unavailable, with a Merck counterpart, CMX counterpart and decision date. Obtain cost/funding proposals before committing campaign spend.

**Reference:** Merck's [analytical learning hub](https://www.sigmaaldrich.com/US/en/life-science/learning-center/analytical-chemistry) and [webinar library](https://www.sigmaaldrich.com/US/en/collections/webinars) provide existing educational material. They do not confirm local support availability or funding.

## Slide 8 Agree the execution calendar and scorecard

**Message:** Agree actions now and validate the forecast through recurring account reviews.

Show quarterly base allocations: Q1 30.96; Q2 31.42; Q3 33.89; Q4 33.38. These reflect 2025 seasonality, not equal quarterly quotas.

Show milestones: 29 January—full-year baseline and 65 account plans; 26 February—qualified opportunity bridge and support decisions; end-Q2—review first program conversions; quarterly thereafter—joint forecast and support review.

Scorecard: revenue versus target, revenue retention, recovery/reactivation revenue, first-observed new-account revenue, Reference Materials first orders, qualified incremental pipeline, and Merck commitment status. A proposed 3× unweighted pipeline-coverage threshold is a planning assumption until actual win rates are known.

**Close with four decisions:** working target; priority programs and owners; Merck support commitments; revenue definition and quarterly review cadence.

## Questions to prepare for

**Is the 104.05 figure total company revenue?** No. It is the Merck-family categorized export through 20 November, after the agreed exclusions.

**Is the baseline fully reconciled?** No. It still needs source checks for document scope, products omitted by category matching, and invoice-line deduplication. Use it provisionally and do not claim quantified missing revenue.

**Why 10% when current growth is 5.6%?** It is an ambition supported by a proposed recovery/expansion allocation, not a trend forecast. Concentration makes validation essential; the target should change if the account bridge cannot support it.

**Are 149 customers lost?** Not proven. They bought during full-year 2025 but have no purchases yet in 2026. Some may purchase late in the year, change account codes or use another channel. Comparable-YTD inactivity is 138 accounts.

**Can we call the 341 Reference Materials gaps opportunities?** They are candidates for qualification. Absence of a recorded purchase does not establish demand, technical suitability or available budget.

**Is new headcount or spending approved?** No. Base assumes current resources; incremental Merck support and campaign funding are proposals to agree.
