# Which Merck categories and subcategories to push in 2027

Decision view for the Merck partner meeting • 20 November 2026

## Recommendation

Put broad commercial effort behind **Analytical Chemistry and Solvents, FB&O Microbiology, and Chemical Synthesis and Discovery Chemistry**. Use **Reference Materials as an attached cross-sell**, rather than assuming its growth is broad-based. Pilot **Cell and General Workflow Tools**. Run a separate recovery program for **Protein and Pathway Analysis, Cell Biology Reagents and Pharma Microbiology**.

This is a revenue-opportunity prioritization based on sales history, buyer breadth, repeat invoices and concentration. It is not a profitability ranking: margins, supply capacity, competitor share and qualified pipeline are not available. Category totals and account opportunities are alternative views of the same business; do not add these initiatives on top of the existing revenue bridge.

## Category direction

| Category | 2027 direction | Where to focus |

Use the source hierarchy: **Biochemistry is recorded under Chemistry**, even though the name might suggest Biology. The small `Solvents (SOL)` bucket is distinct from `Analytical Chemistry & Solvents` in the export; do not silently merge them.

### 1 Analytical Chemistry and Solvents

**Why:** This is the largest established revenue pool, with 500 buyers and 341 repeat buyers. Growth remains 12.4% when the largest current buyer is removed from both comparison periods. That supports expansion, while the broader portfolio still requires protection of PTT and 3M gains.

**Push:** Start with the applications and products already reordered by existing accounts. Ask each priority account which adjacent analytical requirements we do not yet supply, and verify technical suitability before creating a combined quote. Qualify Reference Materials in the same discussion rather than running an unrelated broad promotion.

**Target and Merck request:** Use current analytical buyers, including laboratory-service and research accounts, while core owners confirm major industrial order plans. Ask Merck for application support and account-specific supply confirmation. In Q1, each of the 20 core-account reviews should either identify a qualified incremental application or document why no expansion is available.

### 2 FB&O Microbiology

**Why:** Growth is 17.5%, the buyer count is rising, and the largest customer contributes only 7.3% of revenue. Growth remains 15.1% excluding that customer. This is a stronger diversified growth signal than the small high-percentage-growth buckets.

**Push:** Qualify recurring microbiology testing requirements and related products against the customer's actual workflow. Start in laboratory services and Food and Beverage: those segments contribute THB 1.17m across 14 buyers and THB 0.82m across 11 buyers respectively. The category also sells into universities and other industries, so do not treat its product label as a customer-sector definition.

**Target and Merck request:** Select ten current repeat buyers for application reviews in Q1, with the current account owner as lead. Request one targeted Merck microbiology session and application support for the resulting qualified opportunities. Track incremental orders and second purchases, not session attendance alone. This is a proposed addition to the support discussion, not a committed resource or additional revenue target.

### 3 Chemical Synthesis and Discovery Chemistry

**Why:** Revenue grew 19.7% and buyers rose from 162 to 189. Growth remains 17.0% without the largest current buyer. However, only 69 buyers have multiple invoices, so the task includes improving repeat purchasing rather than extrapolating every first order.

**Push:** Identify applications behind recent purchases and qualify the next project or repeat requirement. Universities contribute THB 0.85m across 60 buyers, laboratory services THB 0.56m across 16, and research centers THB 0.40m across 14. These are observed customer groups, not claims about external market growth.

**Target and Merck request:** Review ten recent or expanding buyer accounts in Q1, selected from the sales history and coordinated with existing account plans. Ask Merck to support application/product selection for verified requirements. Measure qualified next orders and repeat purchases; do not assume a new product basket is appropriate without customer confirmation.

### 4 Reference Materials

**Why:** Revenue is up, but buyers fell from 193 to 185; growth excluding the largest current buyer is only 1.7%. This supports a qualified cross-sell program rather than a claim of strong broad-based expansion. Laboratory-service buyers account for THB 1.54m across 23 accounts.

**Push:** Qualify the 15 selected candidates from the 341 analytical buyers without a recorded Reference Materials purchase. Confirm analyte, method and required material specification with the customer and Merck specialist. A missing purchase is not proof of demand, and materials must be selected for the actual application.

**Target and Merck request:** Retain the two proposed analytical workflow sessions and ask Merck to support the qualified opportunities. Track first Reference Materials orders, incremental revenue and repeat orders. Review the eight-buyer net decline as well as cross-sell acquisition.

### 5 Cell and General Workflow Tools

**Why:** Growth is 53.4% and remains 28.9% without the largest customer, but only 16 of 53 buyers have multiple invoices. The largest account contributes 22.0%. This is promising but less proven as recurring demand.

**Push:** Run a small pilot in university and research-center accounts, which account for THB 1.07m and THB 0.60m respectively. Select five current buyers, establish the actual workflow and identify a customer-confirmed follow-on requirement. Scale only after the pilot generates incremental orders or evidence of repeat demand. Do not make a nationwide campaign or large inventory commitment from the 53.4% growth rate alone.

### 6 Material Science and Flavors and Fragrances

Growth remains positive without the largest account, but the buyer base shrank from 91 to 87. Keep this as selective expansion through existing account plans; diagnose inactive buyers and separate the underlying applications before treating the combined label as one campaign theme. It ranks behind the broader growth priorities for new sales effort.

## Recovery priorities are different from growth campaigns

| Recovery priority | 2026 revenue | YTD decline | Buyer change | Required response |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Protein and Pathway Analysis | 5.27 | −1.18 / −18.3% | 144 → 145 | Recover spend and applications at existing accounts; buyer count alone is not the problem |
| Cell Biology Reagents | 4.36 | −0.46 / −9.5% | 131 → 142 | Identify why average spend fell despite more buyers; review order timing, products and lost applications |
| Pharma Microbiology | 2.00 | −0.59 / −22.8% | 56 → 64 | Diagnose project timing, account mix and supply/commercial issues before committing a broad promotion |

Use the existing Biology account list for the first two priorities and validate Pharma Microbiology declines by account and product. Recovery reasons are not available in invoice data. A category decline does not establish a competitor loss or a pricing problem.

**Biology classification is a material prerequisite:** the unclassified Biology subcategory fell from THB 5.22m to THB 3.36m, a THB 1.85m decline—about 59% of Biology's THB 3.15m net decline. Do not assign that unexplained decline to Protein or Cell Biology. Ask Merck to help map the products, retain the revenue in totals, and revisit the recovery allocation after classification. Resolving labels will not itself generate revenue. The [missing Biology items list](biology_missing_items.md) identifies all current-year SKUs and the missing lookup fields.

## Maintain or qualify individually

- **Molecular Reagents and Tools:** THB 6.49m, +2.0%, 156 buyers. Growth excluding the largest current customer is −2.6%. Protect the installed customer base and qualify attached opportunities; it is not a leading broad growth campaign.
- **Biochemistry, under Chemistry:** THB 6.53m, −1.2%. Maintain existing applications and investigate declines. The largest customer contributes 22.1%, and excluding it revenue fell 11.2%.
- **Analytical Chromatography:** +440.8% growth is misleading as a scaling signal. Revenue is only THB 0.50m, there are four buyers, no repeat buyers, and one customer contributes 93.4%. Treat as individual opportunities.
- **Solvents (SOL):** THB 0.15m and six buyers, with 76.4% from one customer. Review its mapping before combining it with the large analytical-solvents business.
- **Stains and Dyes:** +47.9% growth falls to +1.2% excluding the largest buyer. Maintain qualified opportunities rather than making it a major new campaign.
- **Other Diagnostics and Regulated Materials:** maintain specialist/application-led opportunities; the present scale and decline do not justify a broad revenue-growth push.

## How to use this in the Merck meeting

Lead with three broad push priorities, one cross-sell program and one controlled pilot. Keep recovery targets separate. Ask Merck to confirm application support for FB&O and synthesis, retain the analytical and Biology support requests, and help resolve the unclassified Biology products.

Do not set subcategory quotas solely by multiplying recent growth rates. Once account/application opportunities are qualified, give each incremental opportunity one category and subcategory and reconcile those amounts to the existing conservative/base/stretch revenue bridge. These recommendations do not increase the THB 129.64m working base target or assume new funding. The extra Q1 reviews and sessions require sales-manager capacity prioritization and Merck agreement.
